# 西頂文化 · XIDING

西頂文化以西頂廣仁宮的視覺識別為基礎，旗下有西頂藝術與西頂學院。西頂時光是會員品牌名稱。

XIDING draws on the visual identity of Xiding Guangren Temple. Xiding Arts and Xiding Academy are its two brands. Xiding Lifestyle is the membership name.

The symbol in this directory comes from the published Xiding identity. The complete guide, logos, tokens and component library are in the [XIDING design system](https://github.com/ksamint/dsys_xiding02).
